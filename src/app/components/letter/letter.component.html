Dear Priya,

I wanted to write you something instead of just saying it out loud, because some things are easier to mean when you put them on paper first. So... Happy Birthday COOKIE. I hope today feels exactly as good as you deserve, which is to say: very.

I don't think I tell you often enough that I like you for exactly who you are, not for some easier, edited version of you. The version that overthinks texts and still sends them anyway. The version that's fiercely loyal, a little too stubborn, and so much sweeter than she probably realizes. The way you care about people, the little things you notice, and the softness you carry even when you try to hide it — it's all part of what makes you so special. You somehow manage to make chaos feel like a good time and the smallest moments feel a little warmer just by being there. I hope you never feel like you have to be any less of yourself around me, because all those little pieces of you, even the ones you might overthink or wish were different, are the very things I'm glad I get to know.

Friendship like ours doesn't happen by accident, and it definitely doesn't stay this easy without someone putting in the work, so thank you for putting in the work. For staying. For being exactly the kind of person worth building a whole silly little website for.

Here's to another year of you being unapologetically yourself. I'll be here for all of it, cookie.

With more love than this letter has room for,
— Akki
